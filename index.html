CDR Cyclic Worker — processing and publishing

Own, end to end, a Go service that processes and publishes completed-charge records (~6,000 a day). It runs as multiple replicas with a lock so exactly one replica publishes from a given collection at a time, and a worker pool scales the processing. Graceful shutdown means no record is lost during deploys, and I handle its production support.

Featured project

TripBeacon — AI road-trip planner

One product built as three services. A Go computation engine (Gin) geocodes places, computes multi-leg and round-trip routes, merges polylines and resolves each route leg in parallel with goroutines and channels, behind swappable routing providers. A NestJS gateway handles JWT auth (including Apple and Google sign-in), MongoDB persistence and trip operations, and a React Native app brings it to iOS and Android. In active development: routing providers (OSRM, Google Maps), weather along the route, budget breakdown, safety scoring and LLM trip narratives are next.
